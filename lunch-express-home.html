We pick it up

Our rider arrives at your door at your chosen time between 7:15 and 8:15 AM and collects the tiffin.

We deliver it to school

The tiffin reaches your child’s school and is handed over before lunch, so it is still warm when they eat.

Questions parents ask

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

What is the one-time bag charge?

Your child gets a bag with their name, class and a QR code. The rider scans it at pickup and at school to make sure the tiffin reaches the right child. You pay once and keep the bag.

Will the food really stay warm?

We deliver straight to school with no extra stops, so the tiffin spends the least time possible on the road. An insulated tiffin helps keep food warmer for longer.

Do I need a special tiffin box?

No. Use your child’s regular tiffin. Make sure the lid closes tightly and the child’s name is written on it.

What if my child is absent or school is closed?

Message us by the evening before and we’ll skip that pickup. Please tell us as early as you can.

What if I’m late with the tiffin?

Our rider waits briefly at your door. If you know you’ll be late, call us and we’ll try to adjust.

How does the free trial work?

Message us your child’s school, class and your locality. We confirm whether we cover your area and arrange your trial.

How do I pay?

We’ll share payment details on WhatsApp when you choose a plan.

Ready to give your child a warm lunch every day?

Message us your child’s school and your locality. We’ll tell you the same day if we can start.
